# Stakeholder Identification — TEKINO

## 1. Purpose

This document identifies the stakeholders of the TEKINO Digital Products E-Commerce System, describes their roles, and summarizes their primary needs and expectations.

Stakeholder identification helps the project team understand whose needs must be considered when defining system requirements and planning development activities.

## 2. Stakeholder Analysis

| ID | Stakeholder | Type | Role | Needs and Expectations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ST-01 | Customer | External, primary | Browses and purchases digital products | Easy product search, accurate product information, a convenient checkout process, secure payments, and order tracking |
| ST-02 | Administrator | Internal | Manages the system and its users | Secure access, product management, order management, inventory visibility, and user management |
| ST-03 | Product Owner | Internal | Defines product goals and prioritizes the Product Backlog | Business value, clear requirements, prioritized user stories, and regular feedback on delivered increments |
| ST-04 | Inventory Staff | Internal | Maintains product stock information | Accurate stock records, easy inventory updates, and visibility into product availability |
| ST-05 | Customer Support Staff | Internal | Helps customers resolve problems | Access to relevant order information, clear order statuses, and the ability to respond to customer inquiries |
| ST-06 | Payment Gateway | External system | Processes online payment requests | Secure payment requests, valid transaction information, and reliable payment status responses |
| ST-07 | Delivery Service | External organization | Receives shipment information and delivers orders | Accurate shipping details and access to the information required for delivery |
| ST-08 | Business Owner | Internal | Oversees business goals and overall performance | Reliable operations, customer satisfaction, sales visibility, and sustainable business growth |

## 3. Stakeholder Classification

### Primary Stakeholders

- Customers, who directly use the shopping features.
- Administrators, who manage the system.
- The Product Owner, who prioritizes product development according to business and user value.

### Operational Stakeholders

- Inventory Staff, who maintain product availability information.
- Customer Support Staff, who assist customers with order-related problems.

### External Stakeholders and Systems

- The Payment Gateway, which handles payment processing.
- The Delivery Service, which handles the delivery of placed orders.
- The Business Owner, who is accountable for business outcomes.

## 4. Stakeholder Needs and Requirement Implications

Stakeholder needs provide an initial basis for identifying system requirements.

- Customer needs lead to requirements for product discovery, shopping carts, checkout, payment, and order tracking.
- Administrator needs lead to requirements for product, inventory, order, and user management.
- Product Owner needs lead to backlog prioritization, Sprint planning, and feedback-based refinement.
- Inventory Staff needs lead to requirements for maintaining product stock information.
- Customer Support needs lead to requirements for accessing order statuses and relevant customer information.
- Payment Gateway needs lead to requirements for secure payment integration and transaction-status handling.
- Delivery Service needs lead to requirements for recording shipping details and delivery status.
- Business Owner needs lead to requirements related to reliability, usability, and business reporting.

These implications are preliminary and will be refined during requirements analysis.

## 5. Relationship to Agile Development

Stakeholders contribute to the Agile development process in different ways.

- The Product Owner prioritizes the Product Backlog and clarifies product requirements.
- Customers and other relevant stakeholders provide feedback on proposed features and delivered increments.
- The development team analyzes requirements, designs the solution, implements features, and tests the results.
- Feedback and changing business needs may lead to Product Backlog refinement and adjustments to future Sprints.

The stakeholder needs documented here will be used as input for the functional and non-functional requirements.

## 6. Assumptions

- TEKINO is initially modeled as a single-vendor e-commerce system.
- The Payment Gateway and Delivery Service are external to TEKINO.
- The roles listed above represent the proposed project scope and may be refined during analysis.
- This document describes the intended system; it does not claim that these features have already been implemented.
