# Sprint 1 Planning — TEKINO

## 1. Sprint Overview

**Sprint:** Sprint 1

**Planned Duration:** 2 weeks

**Sprint Goal:**

Enable customers to discover digital products and view the information needed to make purchasing decisions.

**Planning Status:** Planned

This Sprint plan is a proposed academic planning artifact. Actual completion must be recorded only after the relevant work has been performed and reviewed.

## 3. Sprint Backlog and Tasks

### US-01 — Browse and Search Products

**Planned Tasks**

- T-01: Review the requirements and acceptance criteria for product browsing.
- T-02: Define the product catalog and search behavior.
- T-03: Specify the expected product search and results states.
- T-04: Prepare the relevant requirements and design documentation.
- T-05: Define test scenarios for product browsing, search, filtering, and no-result behavior.
- T-06: Review the results against the acceptance criteria.

**Status:** To Do

### US-02 — View Product Details

**Planned Tasks**

- T-07: Review the product-detail requirements and acceptance criteria.
- T-08: Identify the product information that must be displayed.
- T-09: Specify product availability and unavailable-product behavior.
- T-10: Prepare the relevant requirements and design documentation.
- T-11: Define test scenarios for product details and product availability.
- T-12: Review the results against the acceptance criteria.

**Status:** To Do

## 4. Expected Sprint Deliverables

By the end of this planned Sprint, the intended deliverables are:

- Reviewed requirements for product browsing and search.
- Reviewed requirements for product details.
- Defined expected system behavior for relevant scenarios.
- Documented test scenarios for the selected user stories.
- Updated project documentation.
- A review record identifying completed work, remaining work, and issues.

For the current coursework, these deliverables focus on requirements analysis and modeling. If working software is also required by the instructor, implementation and executable testing tasks must be added to the Sprint Backlog.

## 5. Definition of Done

A selected work item may be marked Done only when:

- Its scope and acceptance criteria have been reviewed.
- The required work has actually been completed.
- The result has been checked against the acceptance criteria.
- Relevant documentation and test scenarios have been updated.
- Identified blocking issues have been resolved or explicitly documented.
- The result is ready for review.

A planned task must not be marked Done merely because it has been described in this document.

## 6. Sprint Review

At the end of the Sprint, the team will review the completed deliverables against the Sprint Goal.

The review will identify:

- Which selected work items satisfy their acceptance criteria.
- Which items remain incomplete.
- What feedback was received.
- Whether the Product Backlog needs to be updated.

## 7. Retrospective

The team will reflect on how the Sprint was conducted and identify improvements for the next Sprint.

Possible discussion topics include requirement clarity, task sizing, estimation, communication, and the quality of documentation.

## 8. Next Steps

The results of Sprint 1 will inform subsequent backlog refinement and planning.

Future Sprints may address customer registration and login, shopping cart management, checkout, and online payment, depending on priority, dependencies, and available capacity.
